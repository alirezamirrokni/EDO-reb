We isolated the alpha update using identical frozen calibrated probes, initial thresholds, budgets and tie-breaking. Configurations were locked on separate validation data before full-task evaluation. At epsilon=0.40, 2/5 settings jointly show a positive Type-II effect, Holm-adjusted significance and validation/order-based Type-I feasibility. Source-bootstrap diagnostics support positive effects and feasibility in 0/5. We report paired effect sizes, CIs, costs, pooled precision and coverage; all rounds and feedback are accounted for. Uncertainty from 30 orders, 200 source resamples and five probe fits is reported separately, with epsilon=0.20/0.60 sensitivity. These are locked reanalyses of historical pools, not untouched-test confirmation. Lower Type-II alone is not a cost-efficiency claim.
