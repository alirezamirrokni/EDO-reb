We ran a controlled ablation on four MATH model/encoder settings (Llama-70B and Qwen-32B; Qwen-1.7B and MiniLM). Both policies use identical frozen logistic scores, initial threshold, budget and tie-breaking; only the alpha update is disabled in the control. Settings were locked using development data before evaluation on 30 paired test orders, including all rounds. At prespecified epsilon=0.40, 2/4 comparisons favor adaptation and 0/4 favor freezing after Holm correction. The table reports effect sizes, paired 95% bootstrap CIs, two-sided sign-flip p-values and costs. We also report all epsilon=0.20/0.60 sensitivities and a development-cost-matched baseline. Inference describes ordering robustness on previously inspected pools, not independent-dataset generalization; lower Type-II alone does not imply greater cost efficiency.
